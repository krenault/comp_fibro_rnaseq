# Methods — public generic cell-stress signature vs the FH experiments

Scripts: `scripts/generic_stress/` (`00_`–`06_`).
Figures: `plots/mammalian_generic_stress/`.
Tables: `results/mammalian_generic_stress/`.

FH experiment differential expression production (StringTie / prepDE / DESeq2) is the parent pipeline
([krenault/comp_fibro_rnaseq](https://github.com/krenault/comp_fibro_rnaseq);
[METHODS_fh_experiments.md](METHODS_fh_experiments.md)). Query tables: `drive_dge/` or
`$DRIVE_DGE`.

The design is: build a four-class cellular stress signature from public data,
hold the FH experiments out of discovery, then ask which FH experiment responses recover that
signature and which do not.

---

## 1. Design

Public discovery and the FH experiments are independent experiments. FH experiment libraries are
never used to define the generic list. Glucose is never an FH experiment comparison.
Rousette is dropped (no temperature or hypoxia files). In-vivo GEO is not in
discovery.

The public panel has heat and hypoxia as *classes*. FH experiment heat or hypoxia
recovering the *full* public list is therefore a matched-class positive
control, not evidence of a class-independent CSR. Honest tests drop the
matched class from the gene set before scoring the FH experiments (section 6).

Join key throughout: human Ensembl gene ID (ENSG, version stripped). Mouse
GEO is mapped to human one-to-one orthologs before meta-analysis.

---

## 2. FH experiment query (held out)

Cultured dermal fibroblasts from 13 mammals; 12 species after dropping
rousette (human, gelada, rat, 13-lined ground squirrel, little brown bat,
honey badger, seal, dolphin, whale, rhino, dromedary camel, bactrian camel).
DESeq2 pairwise, design `~ individual + treatment`, human one-to-one
orthologs. Contrasts used here:

- heat: 41°C vs 37°C
- cold: 32°C vs 37°C
- hypoxia: 6 h and 24 h vs 0 h

Glucose (2.5 mM and 30 mM vs 8 mM) is excluded from every FH experiment axis in this
analysis. Files: `drive_dge/` (48 non-glucose, non-rousette contrasts).

## 3. Public discovery panel

Four stressor classes: heat, hypoxia, oxidative (H₂O₂), DNA damage (UV / IR).

**ASTRA.** Human cell-stress atlas, Zenodo 15885686 (Quintanilla et al.).
Tables `datasets.tsv` and `differential_expression.tsv`. Restricted to the
four classes above. Each ASTRA `record_id` is one contrast (dose / time / cell
type kept uncollapsed at ingest, then collapsed by GSE in the mixed model).

**Extra GEO.** Targeted fill for mouse cells and a few human fibroblast
series with usable counts or abundance tables. This was a catalog plus
manual GEO search, not an API sweep. Series that were the FH experiments, in vivo,
cold, OGD/R, or otherwise off-class were recorded as rejected in
`results/mammalian_generic_stress/other_species_candidates.tsv`.
Mouse fibroblast hypoxia added after screening: **GSE328685** (WT tendon
fibroblasts, 1% vs 20% O2 for 4 days) and **GSE282023** (WT immortalized
fibroblasts, 1% vs 21% 24 h; author-normalized counts, SE inflated 1.5×).
Dropped from discovery after design review: GSE171871 (mESC mesoderm
specification), GSE114086 (C2C12 differentiation under hypoxia), GSE225095
(IMR90 H2O2 senescence vs proliferating), GSE66286 (author UVC table, not
genome-wide), GSE205381 (n=1; arsenite / 0.1% O2). GSE179848 (chronic 3%)
is held as a separate sensitivity. The FH experiments are never added to discovery.
A leave-one-series sensitivity drops GSE328685 (`DROP_GSE=GSE328685`;
`scripts/generic_stress/07_sensitivity_compare.R`). That series is a 4-day
hypoxia, not a 6–24 h pulse; primary discovery keeps it, and the sensitivity
asks whether membership, sign, or FH experiment hold-out GSEA calls move when it is
removed.

Discovery size is regenerated by `02_public_hmp.R` after ingest (method
frozen; gene count is not). Pre-expansion freeze: 108 contrasts / 59 GSE
and 1,344 generic genes
(`mammalian_generic_genes_pre_fibro_hypoxia.csv`).

Author-supplied DE-only tables and n = 1 abundance contrasts have their
standard errors inflated 1.5× so they cannot dominate ASTRA.

## 4. Effect sizes and mixed-effects meta-analysis

Adapted from Tyshkovskiy, Gladyshev et al., *Cell* 2023 (mammalian aging
meta-analysis), applied to cellular stress.

1. Per contrast, effect size (EC) is log2 fold-change. Standard error is
   taken from the DE table when present, otherwise backed out from the
   two-sided *p*-value via the normal quantile; if neither exists, a
   conservative MAD-based SE is used.
2. Pairwise Spearman correlations on the union of each contrast’s top 250
   genes (denoised similarity; diagnostic, not the signature rule).
3. Multiple Deming regression rescales ECs across contrasts (L-BFGS-B, 10
   random starts).
4. Per gene, two-stage random-effects: inverse-variance collapse of records
   within GSE, then `metafor::rma` (REML, DerSimonian–Laird or equal-effects
   fallback) across GSE. A gene needs ≥3 contrasts in a class model.
5. Class signatures (heat, hypoxia, oxidative, DNA damage), plus human-only,
   mouse-only, and global models, are genes with BH-adjusted mixed-model
   *p* < 0.05.

The *Cell* paper’s full `rma.mv` with crossed tissue/species intercepts is
too slow for ~15k genes × 15 signatures. Univariate REML on GSE-level
estimates still uses SE. REML vs DL concordance and fibroblast/cancer
Jaccard overlap are optional sensitivity (`scripts/generic_stress/06_reml_celltype.R`;
figure `reml_celltype_overlap`).

## 5. Generic signature (harmonic mean *p*)

HMP is the harmonic mean *p*-value (Wilson, *PNAS* 2019), used as in the
*Cell* 2023 STAR Methods. It combines the four *class* mixed-model
*p*-values for each gene, not the raw contrast *p*-values.

Each class *p* is already two-sided, so it does not remember direction. The
observed sign assigns half of that *p* to the matching direction and the other
half to the opposite direction. The up halves are combined, the down halves
are combined, and the smaller combination is kept. That number is doubled
because both directions were tried, then capped at 1. The only implementation
is `harmonicmeanp::p.hmp` with
equal weights and `multilevel = FALSE` (`share/hmp_combine.R`). With that
setting the package ignores `L` and combines the class *p*-values the gene
actually has. The run stops if `harmonicmeanp` is not installed. A commented
walkthrough of the same function is `share/hmp_combine_annotated.R`.

With 3 or 4 tests, the 80% sign rule is unanimity: 3 of 4 is 75%, and 2 of 3
is 67%. A saved class-model table can be recombined without refitting:

```bash
Rscript share/hmp_combine.R class_tests.csv hmp.csv --L 4
```

`class_tests.csv` is one row per gene per class, with columns `gene`, `class`,
`p`, and `effect`.

A gene is **generic** if:

- BH-adjusted HMP < 0.05
- measured in ≥ 3 of 4 classes
- ≥ 80% of those classes agree on sign

Current pan-cell list after QC drops: **894 genes (534 up, 360 down)**; Jaccard vs the
1,344 freeze is 0.57 (810 shared). Dropping GSE328685 as a sensitivity gives
882 genes (Jaccard 0.95 vs primary, 0 sign flips). The stricter both-species
list is empty after the QC drops: mouse hypoxia and mouse DNA damage each
have only two GSE, so a mouse-only four-class HMP is not identified.
Fibroblast-only HMP on the same pool is **668 genes (283 up, 385 down)**;
Jaccard vs pan-cell is 0.04 (57 shared).

This is not “genes that move in every stressor.” Class-restricted landmarks
(HSPA1A/B, HIF1A, HMOX1, VEGFA, FASN) fail the HMP / sign-agreement rule
and are not labelled generic.

## 6. Pathway analysis of the public generic list

Genes are ranked by −log10(HMP) × sign(mean class EC). GSEA
(`fgseaMultilevel`) uses MSigDB Hallmark, Reactome, and GO biological
process (min size 15, max 400). Table:
`results/mammalian_generic_stress/cell_meta_gsea_hallmark_reactome_gobp.csv`.
Locked figure `public_pathways` plots the three collections in one ranking (shape =
collection); redundant cell-cycle GO terms are collapsed for display.

## 7. Honest FH experiment scoring (class hold-out)

Leave-one-class-out HMP, using the already-fit class mixed models (no
refitting):

| Gene set | Classes combined | Min classes | Scored on |
|----------|------------------|-------------|-----------|
| full | heat + hypoxia + oxidative + DNA damage | 3 | FH experiment cold |
| no_heat | hypoxia + oxidative + DNA damage | 2 | FH experiment heat |
| no_hypoxia | heat + oxidative + DNA damage | 2 | FH experiment hypoxia 6 h and 24 h |
| unmatched | oxidative + DNA damage only | 2 | all FH experiment exposures (shared core) |

Same BH HMP < 0.05 and ≥ 80% sign as the primary list.

Each FH experiment species × exposure is ranked by −log10(*p*) × sign(LFC) and
tested by GSEA against the matching hold-out up/down sets. Call: NES > 0 and
FDR < 0.05 = induced the public up-set; NES < 0 and FDR < 0.05 = opposite.

Cold is the clean test (no public cold in discovery). Heat after dropping
public heat, and hypoxia after dropping public hypoxia, are the honest
matched-class tests. Unmatched (oxidative + DNA damage only) is a sensitivity
that holds the gene set fixed across every FH experiment contrast; it is not the
primary signature.

## 8. Conserved FH experiment genes vs the public generic

Within each FH experiment exposure, a gene is conserved if |median LFC| ≥ 0.25
across species, called in ≥ 5 species, and ≥ 80% of those species share
direction. Overlap with the public generic list is the generic slice; the remainder
is residual to the public list. Figure `conserved_overlap`.

---

## 9. Per-species cross-perturbation HMP

Same combination rule as section 5, applied *within one species in the FH experiments*.
Heat, cold, and hypoxia are three separate arms. Six-hour and 24 h hypoxia
are run as two tests, not as substitutes for each other:

- heat + cold + 24 h hypoxia → `drive_species_hmp_heat_cold_24h.csv`
- heat + cold + 6 h hypoxia → `drive_species_hmp_heat_cold_6h.csv`
- genes that pass both, in the same direction → `drive_species_hmp_both_durations.csv`

Glucose dropped. Rousette dropped. A missing 24 h row is left missing.

A gene is a species cross-perturbation hit if BH HMP < 0.05, ≥ 3 arms
measured, and ≥ 80% sign agreement. The *p*-values combined here are that
species' own DESeq2 *p*-values, not the public class mixed models. BH is
done separately inside each species. With three arms, 80% sign agreement
means all three signs match.

Conservation of those hits across the 12 species in the FH experiments is computed for each
duration test and for the genes that pass both. Count how many
species call the gene, and require ≥ 80% of those calls to share direction.
Headline conserved core: **≥ 5 species**. Over-representation
(`fgsea::fora`) of the both-durations core, split up/down, uses Hallmark +
Reactome + GO BP with universe = genes tested in either species HMP.

## 11. What this analysis does not do

- The FH experiments are not used to discover the generic list.
- Glucose is not an FH experiment comparison.
- In-vivo GEO is not in discovery.
- The primary signature is not fibroblast-only.
- Vote / median-z is not the paper method.
- Adding more same-class GEO, or pulling in ER stress, would rewrite the
  generic list; that is a new analysis, not a sensitivity of this one.

## 12. How to run

```bash
# Skip ingest if ASTRA + extra GEO are already on disk
bash scripts/generic_stress/00_run.sh

# Re-download ASTRA / extra GEO
bash scripts/generic_stress/00_run.sh --ingest
```

`01` ingest, `02` signature, `03` FH experiment query, `04` figures, `05`
per-species FH experiment HMP, `06` REML / cell-type Jaccard. `02` refits the mixed
models from the long table unless `RESUME_MIXED=1` (and `RESUME_SIGS=1` to
also skip the class `rma`). `05` recomputes the species HMP unless
`SKIP_SPECIES_HMP=1`. Shareable combination only, no metafor:
`share/hmp_combine.R`.

Paths are relative to this analysis folder. Override with `FLEX_ROOT` and
`DRIVE_DGE` (`config.example.env`). Software: see `R_PACKAGES.txt`
(tidyverse, DESeq2, metafor, fgsea, msigdbr, harmonicmeanp, AnnotationDbi /
org.Hs.eg.db, babelgene).
